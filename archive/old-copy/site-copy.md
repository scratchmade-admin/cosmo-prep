## FAQ

### Is this the official California state board website?
No. This is independent exam prep designed to help students prepare for the California cosmetology written exam.

### Is this only for cosmetology?
Yes for now. More license tracks can be added later.

### Does this include practice questions?
Yes. The goal is to combine study lessons with quizzes, full mock exams, and answer explanations.

### Can I study on my phone?
Yes. The site should be built mobile-first.

### How long should I study before the test?
That depends on your starting point, but most students benefit from a structured 3-day or 7-day review plan plus repeated practice questions.
