## Sample Questions

### 1. Sanitation
What is the main purpose of cleaning?
A. To destroy all microbial life
B. To remove visible dirt and debris
C. To sterilize implements
D. To replace disinfecting

Answer: B
Explanation: Cleaning removes visible debris. It is usually the first step before proper disinfection.

### 2. Law and Safety
When should a cosmetologist refuse or postpone a service?
A. When the client asks many questions
B. When the client has signs of a contagious condition in the service area
C. When the salon is busy
D. When the service takes too long

Answer: B
Explanation: A service should not be performed when there are signs of a contagious condition, open sores, or other unsafe contraindications.

### 3. Hair Structure
Which layer of the hair is primarily responsible for strength and color?
A. Cuticle
B. Cortex
C. Medulla
D. Follicle

Answer: B
Explanation: The cortex is the middle layer and is responsible for strength, elasticity, and color.

### 4. Haircutting
A 90-degree haircut generally creates:
A. A blunt one-length shape
B. Uniform layers
C. No movement
D. Maximum weight at the perimeter

Answer: B
Explanation: A 90-degree elevation is generally associated with uniform layering.

### 5. Chemical Services
Why is overlapping relaxer onto previously relaxed hair dangerous?
A. It improves smoothness too much
B. It increases shine
C. It can cause breakage and chemical damage
D. It improves elasticity

Answer: C
Explanation: Overlapping relaxer can overprocess the hair and cause serious breakage.

### 6. Color Theory
Which color neutralizes yellow tones?
A. Green
B. Violet
C. Blue
D. Red

Answer: B
Explanation: Violet is opposite yellow on the color wheel and is used to neutralize yellow tones.

### 7. Nail Care
Which part of the nail unit is responsible for nail growth?
A. Free edge
B. Matrix
C. Plate surface only
D. Cuticle oil

Answer: B
Explanation: The matrix is the growth area that produces nail cells.

### 8. Test Strategy
If two answers both seem possible on the exam, the better choice is usually the one that is:
A. Faster for the salon
B. More profitable
C. Safer, more sanitary, and more professional
D. More aggressive

Answer: C
Explanation: Safety, sanitation, and professional scope are strong clues on licensing exams.
