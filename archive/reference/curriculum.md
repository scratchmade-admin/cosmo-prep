# Curriculum Outline

## Module 1: Infection Control and Sanitation
Topics:
- cleaning vs disinfection vs sterilization
- bacteria, viruses, fungi basics
- universal precautions
- blood exposure procedure
- porous vs nonporous items
- single-use items
- handwashing and PPE
- workstation sanitation

Outcome:
Student can identify the safest and most correct sanitation action in salon scenarios.

## Module 2: California Law and Professional Safety
Topics:
- scope of practice
- when to refuse service
- license requirements and display
- consumer protection basics
- professional conduct
- unsafe conditions and contraindications

Outcome:
Student understands how to choose the legal and safe action in client situations.

## Module 3: Hair and Scalp Care
Topics:
- hair structure
- hair growth cycle
- scalp analysis
- common hair and scalp conditions
- pH basics
- porosity and elasticity basics

Outcome:
Student understands foundational hair science likely to appear on the exam.

## Module 4: Shampooing, Conditioning, and Draping
Topics:
- client preparation
- draping for safety and cleanliness
- shampoo procedure basics
- scalp massage basics
- conditioner types and purpose
- contraindications

Outcome:
Student can identify proper, safe service setup and procedure.

## Module 5: Haircutting and Hairstyling
Topics:
- sectioning
- guidelines
- elevation
- overdirection
- tension
- blunt cuts, graduation, uniform layers, long layers
- thermal styling safety
- blow-dry and iron safety

Outcome:
Student can connect haircutting terms and angles to the correct result.

## Module 6: Chemical Services
Topics:
- haircolor types
- developers
- patch testing
- strand testing
- color theory basics
- lightening
- permanent waving
- relaxers
- chemical safety and overprocessing risks

Outcome:
Student can answer chemical-service questions using safe reasoning and product knowledge.

## Module 7: Skin Care and Nail Care Basics
Topics:
- skin layers
- basic facial safety
- contraindications
- nail structure
- manicure safety
- infection-control issues for skin and nails

Outcome:
Student can identify safe practice and basic structures in skin and nail questions.

## Module 8: Anatomy, Chemistry, and Test Strategy
Topics:
- basic anatomy terms
- chemistry vocabulary
- solutions, suspensions, emulsions
- acids and alkalis
- oxidation-reduction basics
- basic electricity and electrical safety
- how to read multiple-choice questions well

Outcome:
Student can handle foundational science questions and improve test-taking accuracy.
