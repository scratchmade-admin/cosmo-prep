3-Day Cram Plan

Three focused days. High-value topics first. Quiz every day to lock it in.

How to use this: Read the module, take the quiz, check your topic breakdown. If a topic still shows below 70%, re-read that section before moving on. Each day takes 45–60 minutes if you stay focused.

Day 1 — High-stakes topics

These three areas make up the largest share of written exam questions. Cover them first so you have two days to reinforce.

Sanitation & Infection Control

Disinfection levels, EPA-registered products, bloodborne pathogens, salon sanitation procedures. High question volume on the real exam.

Read Module 1 →

California Law & Professional Safety

Board of Barbering and Cosmetology rules, license requirements, scope of practice, OSHA basics. Expect several law questions.

Read Module 2 →

Chemical Services

Perms, relaxers, color theory, developer volumes, and chemical safety. One of the most tested and most failed topic areas.

Read Module 6 →

Day 1 quiz check

After reading, take a full mixed quiz. Look only at your scores for Sanitation, Law & Safety, and Chemical Services. Any topic below 70% — go back and re-read that section tonight.

Take the Quiz

Day 2 — Supporting topics

These fill out the rest of the exam. Solid coverage here separates a passing score from a strong one.

Hair Fundamentals

Hair structure, growth cycles, scalp conditions, porosity, and elasticity. Foundational knowledge that supports chemical service questions too.

Read Module 3 →

Shampooing & Draping

Proper draping procedures, shampoo types, scalp massage technique, and sanitation during services.

Read Module 4 →

Haircutting & Styling

Basic cut lines, elevation, sectioning, and thermal styling fundamentals. Lower question volume but still tested.

Read Module 5 →

Day 2 quiz check

Take another mixed quiz. This time focus on Hair Fundamentals, Shampoo & Draping, and Haircutting. Also revisit any Day 1 topic still under 70%.

Take the Quiz

Day 3 — Finish and lock it in

Cover the remaining topics, then do a full review pass on anything still shaky. Day 3 is about consolidation, not new material.

Skin Care & Nail Basics

Skin anatomy, common conditions, facial services scope, and basic nail care. Lighter coverage on the exam but worth a read-through.

Read Module 7 →

Anatomy, Chemistry & Test Strategy

Bones, muscles, skin layers, basic chemistry, and how to approach tricky written-exam questions. The test strategy section alone is worth reviewing.

Read Module 8 →

Day 3 final check

Take one more quiz. This time treat it like the real exam — no pausing, no looking things up. Check your results and write down any topic still under 80%. Spend the last 20 minutes re-reading only those sections.

Take the Final Quiz

You've done the work. Trust it.

Three days of focused review is more effective than a week of scattered studying. Go into the exam knowing you covered the right material in the right order.
