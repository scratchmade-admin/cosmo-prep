FAQ and disclaimer

Common questions

Short version: this is independent prep for the California written exam. Useful, hopefully. Official, definitely not.

Is this the official California state board website?

No. This is an independent exam-prep resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the California Board of Barbering and Cosmetology or any government agency.

Is this only for the written cosmetology exam?

Yes. This version is focused on written-exam prep for California cosmetology students.

Do I need a textbook to use it?

No. It is designed to work as a standalone prep tool, though some students may still want to use it alongside school materials.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The layout is meant to stay simple and readable on mobile.

Does it include practice questions and explanations?

Yes. Practice questions and answer explanations are a core part of the product direction.

Does the study material touch on electricity?

Yes. Module 8 covers basic electrical safety — conductors vs. insulators, grounding, GFCI outlets, and when not to use electrical tools. It is a supporting topic, not a standalone module, but the key concepts are explained and tested in the quiz.

Who is this best for?

Students who want a more organized study path, repeat test takers, and anyone who wastes time bouncing between scattered resources.

Important disclaimer

This website is an independent study resource for students preparing for the California cosmetology written licensing exam. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, approved by, or operated by the California Board of Barbering and Cosmetology or any government agency. Students should confirm current licensing requirements, policies, and official exam information with the appropriate California authority.
